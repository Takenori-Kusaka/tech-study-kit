# 調査の担当への指示（雛形）

```
Research topic <番号>: <論点> for a Japanese guide on <テーマ> for a newly appointed tech lead.
Write E:\Github\<slug>-guide\research\notes\<番号>_<短い名前>.md in Japanese.

Scope: <ユーザーの言葉の論点>. Products: <製品の一覧>. Research date: <日付>.
Sources, in priority order: standards text with section numbers (RFC/W3C/OpenID/IANA), public authorities
(NIST SP/IR, IPA, デジタル庁, ENISA, CISA/CSRB, OWASP ASVS/Cheat Sheets, Thoughtworks Radar), official
product docs (record the page date), primary incident reports/CVEs. Mark drafts with their version and
never use them as the sole basis of a conclusion. State whether an authority document is normative or a
recommendation, and for whom.

Structure: 1) 問い 2) 標準の定め（節番号） 3) 製品ごとの違い（表、公式文書の URL と日付） 4) 事故・失敗例
5) 推奨と根拠 6) 未確定・要確認 7) 結論（スライドに書くこと、箇条書き） 8) 出典（URL と節）.
Quote at most 15 words from any source; otherwise summarise in your own words.
Report back in under 200 words: the key conclusions and anything uncertain.
```
